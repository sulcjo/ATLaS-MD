1. The 298-CV census on r7

Every contact-CV parameterization (5 atom selections × seps 2/3/4 × 14 (r₀,β) sets, residue-balanced, rational switches, strand packing) plus 100+ geometric/torsion/SASA/reference descriptors, scored by spread, normalized entropy, bimodality, BC, and resolvable windows at the campaign k ceiling. Winner: bb r₀=10. The legacy default (r₀=4.5, β=6) is verifiably degenerate. Redundancy: residue-balanced = atom-pairs; Rg = contacts; H-bond counters dead on implicit banks. Note: genpept_r7_cv_* figures use the full extended census.

2. CV2 deep dive: PCA, residualization, diffusion content

Bank torsion PCA (canonical interleaved sin/cos), residualization against CV1, loadings, and two honesty checks: the axes are non-diffusive on real dynamics (Hess C₁ ≈ 0.07) and do not transfer across banks. The residual axis is the physically interpretable one (turn/strand-ψ flip); plain PC2 became the adopted scalar for robustness reasons.

4. Manifold mathematics: diffusion maps, TwoNN, Fourier

Nonlinear machinery instead of hand-picked distances: diffusion-map spectra on two metrics, intrinsic dimension (TwoNN), circular Fourier harmonics per torsion, winding signal scan with a permutation null, and Fisher–Lee circular correlations. Findings: CV1 = the bank's first diffusion coordinate (LASSO R² 0.985); the manifold is ~4-D; interior ψ are bimodal (the curl); no other periodic signals.
