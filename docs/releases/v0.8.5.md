# ATLaS-MD v0.8.5

ATLaS-MD v0.8.5 targets slow convergence along coordinates the umbrellas do not restrain.

On chignolin_9, a frame-level analysis of the whole campaign showed:

- Local structure and the CV2 profile settled after about 5.5 µs.
- The folded/native populations kept rising until the 13.2 µs pool ran out.
- The D3/P4 turn dihedrals barely move along a trajectory (autocorrelation 0.93 at 10 ns), while every epoch re-pulled all windows from seeds, so continuous trajectories lasted only ~14 ns.

This release keeps trajectories continuous across epochs and makes a stronger, safe peptide boost possible.

## Highlights

- **Continue existing states between epochs** (`--ap-continue-states`, off by default).
  - Every state that already ran continues from its newest end state.
  - Only states the adaptive machinery adds are seeded and pulled.
  - This removes the per-epoch re-pull of every window (about 1 h 50 min of wall time per epoch at 212–236 windows on one GPU), along with its reset of slow-coordinate memory.
- **Pep-GaMD FSF clamp** (`--pep-gamd-fsf-floor-total/-dihedral`, off by default).
  - Below a floor the boost continues linearly, so boosted forces never reverse or collapse.
  - With it, σ0 = 10 kcal/mol is safe at 3.5 fs: on the chignolin_10 envelope, k0_Total went from 0.36 to 0.60.
  - The floors are frozen into the envelope and read by the integrator, every reweighting path and the NPT barostat alike.
- **Restraint-aware seeding.** Seed choice and the start-quality gate ignore the placeholder centre of an unrestrained axis.

## Changed — results-changing

- **The campaign default edge metric is now `pairwise-mbar`.** Pass `--ap-edge-metric marginal` to keep the old behaviour.
- **Restraint-aware seed scoring** can choose a different (better) seed for windows that restrain only one CV.
- **The start-quality gate** no longer fails unrestrained-axis windows.

## Fixed

- **Chain-end PDBs.** PDB seeds now come from each window's chain end, not the last file in name order. This also affected frozen-final extensions: on chignolin_9, 208/236 windows of `final_extension_002` started from older forks.
- **FSF clamp edge cases** found in adversarial review: an uninitialised envelope gave NaN, and sidecar runs didn't carry the floors.
- **CV2 verification F1–F3**, the edge-metric re-grade after the union, and the respring/ladder chaining fix.
