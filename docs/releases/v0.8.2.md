# ATLaS-MD v0.8.2

ATLaS-MD v0.8.2 is the official release of the adaptive CV-selection and thermodynamic-repair work developed after v0.8.1.

## Highlights

- Ab initio automatic CV2 selection from swarm/GENPEPT evidence, without requiring a native structure or supervised folded/unfolded labels.
- A compiled residual-torsion coordinate used consistently by fitting, OpenMM deployment, sampling, exchange cross-pricing, reprojection, and stored artifacts.
- Versioned kernel identity and fail-closed eligibility rules for residual-CV equilibrium data.
- Region-aware adaptive layouts preserving an exactly unrestrained state and representatives of discovered conformational regions.
- Collision-aware solvent repair and transactional seed grafting.
- Swarm-to-production handoff of the frozen GaMD envelope, preventing silent recalibration in production.
- Reproducible high-replica launcher guidance for the 248-state chignolin_8 campaign.
- The new ATLaS-MD molecular-cartography visual identity and landing hero.

## Compatibility note

The residual-CV kernel and artifact schema changed after v0.8.1. Residual-CV segments that cannot demonstrate the current evaluator/exchange-kernel identity are classified as affected or unknown and are excluded from equilibrium analysis by default. Conventional and non-residual data retain their existing eligibility rules.

The recorded thermodynamic-repair completion run reported 3374 passed, 2 skipped, and 7 pre-existing failures; the 47-file touched/new targeted batch reported 835 passed.
